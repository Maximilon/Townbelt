Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Townbelt has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page sets out our standards, how to report concerns, and what we do when we receive a report.

1. What we prohibit

Child sexual abuse and exploitation means any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children. On Townbelt, it is strictly prohibited to:

  • Share, request or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM), meaning any image, video, drawing or text that sexualises a minor, including AI-generated or altered material.
  • Groom a child, meaning to build a relationship with a minor in order to sexually abuse or exploit them.
  • Sextort anyone, meaning threaten to share intimate images, or demand them, money or other favours.
  • Engage in child trafficking or any sexual solicitation of a minor.
  • Sexualise minors in any other way, or promote, praise or normalise child sexual abuse.

Townbelt is only for people aged 13 and over. We remove accounts belonging to anyone younger when we become aware of them.

2. How to report in the app

  • A post: tap the ⋮ menu on the post and choose Report Post.
  • A comment: open the post, tap the ⋮ menu next to the comment and choose Report Comment.
  • A person: open their profile, tap the ⋮ menu at the top and choose Report.

You can also email max@townbelt.com with the subject "Child safety report". Please don't attach or forward any abusive images. Just tell us the person's name or describe the post, and we'll find it.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

3. What we do

  • We review child safety reports as our highest priority.
  • We remove content that breaks these standards as soon as we're aware of it, and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report child sexual abuse material and any child exploitation we find to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to relevant law enforcement, and we preserve the information they need to investigate.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

4. Compliance with the law

Townbelt complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the obligation to report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities.

5. Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety, including questions from Google Play and law enforcement, is Max Baddeley: max@townbelt.com.
