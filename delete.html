Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Townbelt account and all of its data at any time, straight from the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Townbelt and sign in.
  2. Go to the Notifications tab.
  3. Tap the Settings gear in the top-right corner.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

What gets deleted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your profile (name, phone number, photo, banner and bio), posts, photos, comments, likes, friendships, introductions and notifications. There's no recovery period, so it can't be undone.

Can't access the app?

Email max@townbelt.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Townbelt account", and include the phone number you signed up with. We'll confirm it's your account, then delete it and everything listed above.
